- **Photographs on the mosque display:** see `mobile/assets/photos/CREDITS.md`.
- **Adhan recordings:** see `mobile/assets/sounds/LICENSES.md`.
- **Prayer time engine:** [adhan-js](https://github.com/batoulapps/adhan-js), MIT.
